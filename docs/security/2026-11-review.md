# Security review, November 2026

A review of the live version (`main`) and the open PRs. Findings by severity, with what changed.

| # | Severity | Finding | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | High | **Taking over the reference face.** The phone reports its own location, so anyone who knew a registered teen's name and birth date could do that teen's first check-in from anywhere and save their own face as the reference. At that point 57 of 114 registrations had never checked in. | Fixed: the first check-in needs the leaders' code of the day (`lib/server/service-code.ts`). |
| 2 | High | **Duplicate registration by another family.** Registering a real teen's name and birth date again made their check-in ambiguous (they couldn't check in), or matched the check-in to the copy and sent the notices, with the photo, to the copy's email. | Fixed: refused with `CADASTRO_PARECIDO`; #2 also flags possible duplicates for review. |
| 3 | Medium | **No limits on the public routes.** Unlimited identification (guessing a teen's birth date from their name), face recognition calls, visitor check-ins with 2 MB uploads and registrations emailing any address through the church's SMTP. | Fixed: limits per network and per person (`lib/server/rate-limit.ts`). |
| 4 | Medium | **Leader lockout.** 8 wrong passwords per email every 15 min locked a leader out from anywhere. | Fixed: per email and network, plus a higher per-email cap. |
| 5 | Medium | **Open `/api/health`.** Anyone could load the face models (CPU and cost) and see the number of active leaders. | Fixed: public answer is only `ok`. |
| 6 | Medium | **No framing protection.** `/pastor` could be framed (clickjacking). | Fixed: `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, CSP `frame-ancestors 'none'`. |
| 7 | Medium | **nodemailer 6.10** with high advisories (mail to an unintended domain; SMTP command injection). | Fixed: nodemailer 10. |
| 8 | Medium | **Data minimization in `main`:** CPF still collected, precise coordinates stored, the creative photo (which may show other teens) emailed, no guardian email verification, no retention. | Fixed in the open PRs #2, #3 and #4 (waiting for review). |
| 9 | Low | **Flat leader privileges.** Any signed-in session could reset another leader and receive their activation code. | Mitigated: account actions ask for the current authenticator code. |
| 10 | Low | **Default table grants** (including `TRUNCATE`, which RLS doesn't cover) for `anon`/`authenticated`. | Fixed: `20261117000000_permissoes.sql`. |
| 11 | Low | **Visitor check-in time leak.** Repeating someone's name and phone returned their arrival time. | Fixed. |
| 12 | Low | **Build-time `postcss`** advisory through Next.js. | Fixed with an `overrides` entry. |
| 13 | Low | **Dev-only tooling** (Vercel CLI's `tar`, older Vitest) with advisories. | Vitest 5. The Vercel CLI's `tar` is still flagged; it never ships to production. |

## Known limits

- The check-in location is still reported by the phone. The code of the day covers the first check-in; later
  check-ins need the teen's face to match.
- Limits count per network as seen by Vercel (`x-forwarded-for`). The church's Wi-Fi shares one address, so those
  limits are generous and the tighter ones are per person.
